Common questions

How do I write a story?
Type exactly six words for today's prompt and tap Save. The counter turns green at six.

Who can see my stories?
Stories you share appear in Share & Compare under your pen name, never your real name. You can turn automatic sharing off in Settings. Stories in a circle are seen only by that circle's members.

How do I report or block someone?
Tap ••• on any story in Share & Compare and choose Report or Block. Reported stories are reviewed, usually within 24 hours.

How do circles work?
Tap Circles, make a circle, and invite people. Ask them to install Six Little Words first, then tap your invite. Members take turns picking the day's prompt.

How do I delete my data?
Open Settings → Delete my data. You can delete everything you've shared, or everything including your bookshelf.

I got a new phone. Where are my stories?
Your bookshelf is backed up to your own iCloud. Sign in to the same iCloud account and open the app; your books come back.
